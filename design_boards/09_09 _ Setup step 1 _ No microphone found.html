Step 1 of 4 · Needs attention

We can’t find a microphone

Aurel needs a mic to listen to. Nothing is plugged in, or Windows is keeping it from apps.

No input devices on this PC Aurel checks again every few seconds. Plug in your mic and this page moves on by itself.

Things that usually fix it

1
Let apps use your microphoneWindows privacy settings can block every app, including Aurel. This is the most common cause.
2
Plug your mic straight into the PCUSB hubs and front-panel ports sometimes don’t give the mic enough power. Try a port on the back.
3
Using an XLR mic? It needs an interfaceXLR mics can’t plug into a PC directly. Connect an audio interface over USB and pick that here.
4
Check it’s enabled in Sound settingsA disabled device is hidden from every app.
